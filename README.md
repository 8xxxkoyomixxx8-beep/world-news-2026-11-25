One image that sums up the world news of Wednesday 25 November 2026, drawn as an editorial front page: the Cairo Ring Road fuel tanker fire that killed at least 31 as the lead story, with Asia's market plunge led by the KOSPI's 6.16% fall, Huawei's conviction in a Brooklyn court on 10 of 12 counts, the Houthi claim against Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura refinery, Ukrainian drone hits on two Rosneft refineries, Sudan's RSF drone strikes, the Australia–Solomon Islands treaty barring foreign bases, and a ticker of the day's smaller stories. draw.py renders the page with PIL from the day's verified headlines; main.py validates the finished PNG (signature, dimensions, size, chunk CRCs) and copies it to the output directory, byte for byte, so repeated runs land on exactly the same file.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
# writes entry.png to /output, or to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR when that is set
```
